Most chaos fiction tells you what will happen. Chaos Simulator tells you what most people will actually do — usually less heroic, often less terrible, almost always different from what the genre predicts. Six disruptions, three social configurations, eighteen honest readings. 大多数混沌虚构告诉你将发生什么。混沌模拟器告诉你多数人实际会做什么——通常没那么英雄、常常没那么糟，几乎总与类型预测不同。六种破坏，三种社会配置，十八份诚实读数。

C1 · Six Disruptions

Six scenarios. Each one breaks something different.六种情境。每一种破坏的东西都不同。

A real disruption is named by what it removes. The genre tends to amplify drama; honest simulation tracks the boring middle, where most adults eventually live.真实破坏由它移除的东西来命名。类型往往放大戏剧性；诚实的模拟追踪无聊的中段——多数成年人最终居住的地方。

C2 · The Simulator

Pick a disruption. Pick a society. Run the year.挑一种破坏。挑一种社会。跑过这一年。

6 disruptions × 3 social configurations = 18 templated futures. The output is a year-long arc, not a doomsday vignette. Honesty: this is templated, not a frontier model.6 种破坏 × 3 种社会配置 = 18 种模板化未来。输出是一段一年长的弧线，不是世界末日小品。坦诚：这是模板化的，不是前沿模型。

C4 · Guardrails

What this simulator refuses to pretend.本模拟器拒绝伪装的事。

Five things Chaos Simulator will not do.混沌模拟器不会做的五件事。

  • No collapse pornography. Most disruptions are not heroic. They are eighteen months of administrative tedium punctuated by short crises. We will not simulate a more dramatic version because the genre rewards drama.不做崩溃色情。多数破坏都不英雄。它们是十八个月的行政沉闷，间杂短暂危机。我们不会因为类型奖励戏剧性而模拟更戏剧化的版本。
  • No prepper fantasy. The simulator does not produce "what to stockpile" guides; in most disruptions, individual stockpiling is statistically irrelevant compared to local network configuration.不做末日囤积者幻想。模拟器不产出"该囤什么"指南；在多数破坏中，与本地网络配置相比，个人囤积在统计上无关紧要。
  • No utopian abundance. Infinite abundance is included specifically to refuse the easy version: most adults under abundance do not become better, more creative, or more generous. They become bored. We will not pretend otherwise.不做乌托邦丰盛。"无限丰盛"被特意纳入以拒绝其简单版本：丰盛之下，多数成年人不会变得更好、更有创造力或更慷慨。他们会变得无聊。我们不假装相反。
  • No real-event prediction. The simulator works on stylised disruptions. It is not a forecasting tool, not an investment thesis, and not a guide to actual events; people who use it as one will lose money or time.不做真实事件预测。模拟器在风格化破坏之上工作。它不是预测工具、不是投资论题、不是真实事件指南；把它当作上述之一使用的人会损失金钱或时间。
  • No infinite-AI-generation slop. Eighteen templated futures + five canned probes + one heuristic fallback is better discipline than infinite plausible-sounding LLM scenario writing.不做无限 AI 生成的污水。18 种模板化未来 + 5 个预设探针 + 1 个启发式回退，比无穷个貌似合理的 LLM 情境写作是更好的纪律。
